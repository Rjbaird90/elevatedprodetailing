Terms of Service

Elevated Pro Detailing Terms.

By accepting an estimate, scheduling an appointment, or purchasing services from Elevated Pro Detailing, you acknowledge and agree to the following Terms of Service.

1. Appointment & Payment

  • Payment is due immediately upon completion of services unless prior arrangements have been made.
  • We accept major credit/debit cards, cash, and other approved payment methods.
  • Prices are based on the condition and size of the vehicle. Excessively dirty vehicles, excessive pet hair, biohazards, heavy staining, sand, mud, or other conditions requiring significantly more labor may incur additional charges. Any additional charges will be discussed with and approved by the customer before work begins.

2. Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • We request at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or appointment changes whenever possible.
  • Elevated Pro Detailing reserves the right to reschedule appointments due to weather, unsafe working conditions, equipment failure, illness, or other unforeseen circumstances.
  • Mobile detailing requires safe working conditions and reasonable access to the vehicle.

3. Vehicle Condition

  • Customers are responsible for removing personal belongings and valuables from the vehicle before service.
  • Elevated Pro Detailing is not responsible for lost, misplaced, or damaged personal items left inside the vehicle.
  • Customers should disclose any pre-existing damage, loose trim, aftermarket modifications, mechanical issues, or delicate surfaces before service.

4. Service Limitations

While we strive to achieve the best possible results, no detail can guarantee the removal of:

  • Permanent stains
  • Deep scratches
  • Paint chips
  • Oxidation
  • Etching
  • Embedded contaminants
  • Worn upholstery
  • Interior discoloration
  • Odors caused by mold, smoke, mildew, or long-term contamination

Results vary depending on the age and condition of the vehicle.

5. Paint Correction & Surface Defects

Paint correction improves the appearance of paint but cannot safely remove every scratch or defect. Deep scratches that extend below the clear coat may remain visible after service.

6. Ceramic Coatings & Protective Products

Protective products, including ceramic coatings, sealants, waxes, and dressings, require proper maintenance to achieve their expected lifespan. Environmental conditions, washing methods, and vehicle usage affect durability. No product lasts indefinitely.

7. Weather & Mobile Service

For mobile appointments:

  • Customers must provide reasonable access to the vehicle.
  • If electrical power or water access is required, this must be available unless otherwise agreed.
  • Severe weather, high winds, lightning, freezing temperatures, or other unsafe conditions may require rescheduling.

8. Satisfaction Guarantee

Customer satisfaction is our priority. If you are dissatisfied with any aspect of your service, please notify Elevated Pro Detailing within 24 hours of service completion. We will make reasonable efforts to address any concerns. Refunds are provided at the sole discretion of Elevated Pro Detailing.

9. Liability

Elevated Pro Detailing is fully insured; however:

  • We are not responsible for pre-existing damage, faded paint, failing clear coat, brittle plastics, cracked leather, loose trim, or previously repaired surfaces that become apparent during normal cleaning.
  • We are not responsible for failures resulting from worn or defective vehicle components.
  • Our liability is limited to the amount paid for the detailing service performed.

10. Photos

Elevated Pro Detailing may photograph vehicles before, during, and after service for documentation, quality control, and marketing purposes. License plates and personal identifying information will not intentionally be displayed. Customers may request that their vehicle not be used in promotional materials before service begins.

11. Biohazards

Vehicles containing bodily fluids, mold, excessive biological contamination, needles, pests, or hazardous materials may be declined or subject to additional fees.

12. Right to Refuse Service

Elevated Pro Detailing reserves the right to refuse or discontinue service if working conditions are unsafe, the vehicle presents a health hazard, or the customer behaves in a threatening or abusive manner.

13. Acceptance

By accepting an estimate, scheduling an appointment, signing an invoice, or submitting payment, the customer acknowledges that they have read and agree to these Terms of Service.
