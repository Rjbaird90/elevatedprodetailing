What service should I choose?

For a full interior and exterior reset, choose Signature Restore. If you only need one side of the vehicle cleaned, choose Interior Detail or Exterior Detail. For faded, hazy, or swirled paint, choose a paint enhancement or paint correction service.

Do you do boats, RVs, motorcycles, or other custom work?

Yes. Elevated Pro Detailing works on boats, RVs, motorcycles, enclosed trailers, convertible tops, and much more. If you have custom work, please contact us for a quote.

Do you offer mobile detailing?

Yes. Elevated Pro Detailing offers mobile service and drop-off appointments. Mobile availability depends on schedule, location, weather, and having a safe space to work around the vehicle.

Do you detail company fleets?

Yes. Elevated Pro Detailing offers fleet detailing and commercial vehicle detailing for commercial fleets, company vehicles, work trucks, and service vans. Company vehicle detailing can be tailored to the number of vehicles, vehicle type, and condition.

Do you offer recurring fleet maintenance?

Yes. Recurring fleet maintenance plans are available for businesses that want vehicles cleaned on a consistent schedule. This can include mobile fleet detailing for work truck detailing, service van detailing, and other commercial vehicle needs when conditions allow.

Can you service vehicles at our business location?

Yes, mobile fleet detailing may be available at your business location depending on schedule, distance, weather, vehicle access, and having a safe space to work around the vehicles.

How is fleet pricing determined?

Fleet pricing is determined by the number of vehicles, vehicle size, service level, vehicle condition, location, and whether recurring service is needed. Custom pricing, volume pricing, and recurring fleet maintenance plans are available by quote.

Where are you located?

Elevated Pro Detailing is based in Fort Atkinson, Wisconsin and serves surrounding Southeast Wisconsin communities including Jefferson, Whitewater, Lake Mills, Watertown, Milton, Edgerton, Oconomowoc, Delafield, Janesville, Madison, and nearby areas.

Is there a travel fee for mobile service?

Drop-off is included in the service price. Mobile service within 0-5 miles is included, 5-20 miles is $25, 20-40 miles is $35, and farther locations can be quoted by request.

How long does a detail take?

Timing depends on the service, vehicle size, and condition. A basic exterior detail is usually faster, while interior details, Signature Restore, pet hair removal, heavy stains, and paint correction require more time. A Signature Restore takes roughly 4-5 hours.

Do I need to remove my belongings first?

Yes, please remove personal items, car seats, paperwork, valuables, trash, and anything you do not want moved. This helps the appointment go smoother and gives more time for the actual detailing work.

Can you remove pet hair?

Yes. Pet hair removal is available as an add-on because it often takes extra time and tools. Severe pet hair may require additional time depending on the upholstery and how embedded the hair is.

Can you remove stains and odors?

Many stains and odors can be improved significantly, but full removal depends on the material, age of the stain, and source of the odor. Heavy stain treatment, carpet shampoo extraction, and odor eliminator options are available as add-ons.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Mobile appointments may need to be rescheduled because of rain, storms, high winds, extreme temperatures, or unsafe detailing conditions. Drop-off appointments may be a better option when weather is unpredictable.

What payment methods do you accept?

Elevated Pro Detailing accepts cash, major cards, American Express, and Venmo. Payment options are also shown on the homepage near the booking section.

What is paint enhancement?

A 1-step paint enhancement is a machine polish focused on boosting gloss and clarity while reducing light swirls, haze, and minor oxidation. It is a great choice for newer vehicles or maintenance polishing.

What is paint correction?

A 2-step paint correction uses a compounding step and a finishing polish to address heavier swirls, oxidation, and defects. It is recommended when you want a deeper correction or are preparing for ceramic coating.
